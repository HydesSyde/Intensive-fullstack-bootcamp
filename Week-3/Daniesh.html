God, the originator of mathematics and all other fields of study, is never for once a dunce and all His works are error-free. There are numerous calculations involved in building a house (Proverbs 24:3) and those who specialise in that field are perceived as intelligent and skillful. Indeed they are. What then do we infer of Him that founded the earth (Proverbs 3:19) when we say in his work of marriage 1+1=1? Behold, nursery pupils will contend with you on that.

God saw the man in the garden that he was alone or rather let us say “all one” (Genesis 2:18). He saw the need for a help for the man and took out of the man that was all one. By so doing, man was no longer whole but a fraction of who he was. That which God took He made into a woman and brought her to the man. Man, now fractionalised, received back what was taken out of him that he may become whole again. Therefore, the calculation in marriage is two fractioned people (man and his wife) joined together to become one.
An example:
2/3 + 1/3 = 1

Note that the equation above is not the same in all marriages because the value of fraction taken out differs from man to man. Thus, the equation is dependent on the value of fraction God takes out of man. He may take out 1/5 of a man from that man and take out 2/7 from another. For a man to get his right fraction he needs to ask from God and seek her in God (Proverbs 18:22, Proverbs 19:14). A man’s true wife is an inheritance from God. The purpose of God in marriage is to make (fractionalised) twain one (not more than nor less than one)(Matthew 19:4-6).

Little wonder Jesus told that woman by the well of Samaria that who she joined herself to is not her husband. Why? Because their union does not make one. A godly seed can only be gotten from this union of “one” and by this simple act of marrying right the world can be a better place (Malachi 2:13-15, Genesis 6:4-5).
