WATER YOUR PLANT

AUTHOR. OLUWATOSIN IROMINI

Short Story

There was a young man who wanted to become successful. He watched his friends get good jobs, buy cars, build houses and achieve things he could only dream of.

Sometimes, he felt left behind. He wondered if life had forgotten him.

One day, an old farmer told him: "When you plant a seed, you don't keep digging it up every morning to check if it's growing. You water it, protect it and give it time."

Those words changed his perspective. He stopped comparing his journey with others. He focused on learning, improving himself, working hard and making the best of every opportunity.

Years later, he became successful, not because he was the fastest, but because he refused to give up.
