Understanding Accessibility in Cybersecurity

Accessibility, in the context of cybersecurity, refers to the principle of ensuring that information,
systems,services are usable and operable by all individuals, including those with disabilities.
This includes making sure that people with disabilities can perceive, understand, navigate,
and interact with digital content and technologies.

Accessibility Matters in Cybersecurity

Accessibility needs to be a priority in cybersecurity because without it, millions of people with disabilities are excluded from safely using digital systems, leaving them more vulnerable to cyber threats and creating inequities in security.

    
Cybersecurity ensures that  everyone regardless of ability can protect their data, identities, and privacy.
Inclusivity: Ensuring that cybersecurity measures do not hinder individuals with disabilities accessing and using technology.
Equity: Providing equal protection and security measures for all individuals, of their abilities.
