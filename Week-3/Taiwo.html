PCOS: The Bigger Picture

----------A closer look at PCOS----------

Did You Know?

You can have PCOS
without having ovarian cysts.

----------◆-----------

PCOS is a hormonal and metabolic condition.
Despite its name, ovarian cysts are not required for a PCOS diagnosis.

What if a condition could affect your skin, weight, hormones and reproductive health,
all at the same time?

That’s what makes Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) more complex than many people realise.

PCOS can influence how the body responds to insulin, regulates hormones
and manages its menstrual cycle. This is why its effects can go beyond reproductive health.

Common Signs and Symptoms of PCOS:

  • Irregular periods
  • Acne or excess hair growth
  • Difficulty getting pregnant
  • Insulin resistance

Three Things To Know:

  1. PCOS is a hormonal and metabolic condition that affects how the body functions.
  2. You can have PCOS without having ovarian cysts.
  3. PCOS symptoms vary from person to person, and not everyone experiences the same signs.
